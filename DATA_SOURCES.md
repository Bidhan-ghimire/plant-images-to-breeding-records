# Data sources and recorded results

The 40 ground images are a demonstration subset of the strawberry breeding-trial dataset by Zhou and colleagues, downloaded from [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.bzkh189nw). The dataset is released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as recorded in the [DataCite metadata](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5061/dryad.bzkh189nw). The README and notebook give the complete article and dataset citations.

The images were previously used in model validation. They demonstrate the image-to-database workflow and are not an independent accuracy test. Counts, detection overlays, and verification results were generated by this demonstration; they are not human-validated ground-truth counts.

Plant and plot identifiers are local demonstration records. Capture dates and plot assignments came from the owner's corrected mapping. The local mapping records 28 November 2023 for images 1–30 and 20 December 2023 for images 31–40; Dryad describes the earlier ground-imaging session as 29 November 2023. This date difference is unresolved, and the supplied mapping is preserved.

The illustrated run was completed on 30 September 2026 using the combined GI + AI5 + AI10 YOLO11x checkpoint identified in [MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md). Dataset photographs and saved prediction-run files are excluded. Obtain photographs from Dryad and select the exact filenames in [data/image_manifest.csv](data/image_manifest.csv). The notebook and README retain three illustrated examples and the recorded demonstration tables.

If you use the trained model, please cite Zhou et al. (2025), the research article, and the Dryad dataset listed in the notebook and README. The model was trained in the linked reproduction project; it is not the authors' original checkpoint.
